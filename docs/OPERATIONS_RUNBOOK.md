# 📘 FRIDAY Production Operations Runbook

This runbook serves as the master operational guide for running, maintaining, troubleshooting, and recovering the **FRIDAY Operating System** and its four managed subsystems (**Trading Bot**, **AI-Universe**, **FORGE Engine**, and **Nexus Website & Growth**).

---

## 📋 1. Daily Operations Checklist

1. **Morning Routine (08:00 UTC)**:
   - Speak *"Morning briefing"* or review the automated executive briefing in `reports/ecosystem/`.
   - Verify portfolio equity, active positions, and overnight P&L across Binance, Bybit, and OKX.
   - Review high-intent leads generated by Nexus and assign follow-up tasks.
   - Check FORGE build pipeline queue and verify test coverage of delivered software packages.
2. **Intraday Monitoring**:
   - Check ecosystem dashboard at `/dashboard` or mobile view at `/mobile`.
   - Verify all 12 persistent operators are in `RUNNING` state.
3. **Evening Routine (20:00 UTC)**:
   - Speak *"Evening wrap-up"* to inspect realized daily P&L deltas and tomorrow's outlook.
   - Verify state backup was generated (snapshots created every 6 hours).

## 🛠️ 2. Weekly & Monthly Maintenance

### Weekly Maintenance (Sunday Evening)
- **Log Rotation**: Ensure log files in `logs/` are rotated and compressed.
- **Backup Verification**: Audit latest backup snapshot in `backups/friday/` and test mock restore.
- **Performance Review**: Execute memory profiling via `FridayDoctorEnhanced` to confirm zero uncollectable garbage objects.

### Monthly Review
- **Quantitative Strategy Audit**: Review profit factors, Sharpe ratios ($>2.0$), and maximum drawdowns ($\le 5.0\%$).
- **API Cost Analysis**: Calculate total token consumption across AI-Universe providers.
- **Security Audit**: Review intrusion detection logs and verify biometric lockout history.

## 🚨 3. Emergency Operating Procedures

### Emergency Procedure 1: Instant Multi-Exchange Trading Halt
- **Voice Command**: *"Emergency stop trading"*
- **Execution Target**: $< 1.0\text{s}$ latency
- **Effect**: Halts bot execution loop, cancels all active orders across all exchanges, releases margin leverage.

### Emergency Procedure 2: Disable All Automation
- **Action**: Execute `operator_manager.pause_all()`
- **Effect**: Suspends all 12 background state machines while maintaining live telemetry monitoring.

### Emergency Procedure 3: Full System State Restoration
- **Action**:
  ```python
  from friday.core.backup_recovery import backup_recovery_manager
  snapshots = backup_recovery_manager.list_snapshots()
  latest_snap_id = snapshots[0]["snapshot_id"]
  backup_recovery_manager.restore_snapshot(latest_snap_id)
  ```
