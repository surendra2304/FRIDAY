Addressed (`hello FRIDAY`) and compound requests (`hey, open notepad`) intentionally fall through to the normal pipeline; the LLM handles conversational greetings via the system-prompt GREETING HANDLING rule.

## Session 5 — Groq 404 Model-Not-Found Universal Fallback

### Daily Summary (Session 5)

Extended `GroqLLMProvider` with a second internal fallback axis: 404 on the primary model now retries the same prompt on the universally available `llama3-8b-8192` (`GROQ_UNIVERSAL_FALLBACK_MODEL`) before raising `LLMProviderError` — which is what lets the outer chain advance to Cerebras.
- Full in-provider cascade: primary (`llama-3.3-70b-versatile`) —429→ fast fallback (`llama-3.1-8b-instant`) —404→ universal (`llama3-8b-8192`); primary —404→ universal. A second 429 still fails fast (Groq rate limits are organization-wide; same-org retries waste quota).
