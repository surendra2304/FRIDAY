## Bugs I Found & Fixed
- **Advisory Decision Invariant**: Predictive engines could be mistaken for autonomous action creators.
  - *Fix*: Strictly isolated `PredictionInformedDecisionEngine` outputs as input context and recommendations, never autonomous executions.
- **Bare Point Estimate Invariant**: Forecasting queries initially risked emitting solitary point numbers without error margins.
  - *Fix*: Formatted all voice responses in `FuturisManagerSkill` and `PredictiveBriefingWorkflow` with explicit calibrated confidence intervals (e.g., `[68% - 82% @ 90% CI]`).
- **Emergency Halt Subsystem Count**: Sequential halt assertion failed after adding Sentinel and IntelX to the halt cascade.
  - *Fix*: Updated halt states verification across all tests to validate the complete 7-subsystem freeze sequence.

## Key Decisions & Architecture
- Structured daily predictive briefings as dedicated debriefs delivered via voice alongside operational and intelligence briefings.
- Probability-Weighted Risk Horizon: Formatted 48h risks with breach probabilities, confidence intervals, and primary causal drivers.
- Predictions As Decision Inputs: Probabilistic forecasts always inform recommendations without usurping operator or autonomous workflow control.
- Mandatory Confidence Intervals: FRIDAY strictly never returns point predictions without calibrated lower/upper bounds.
- Registered `futuris` as autonomous forecasting subsystem in `EcosystemRegistry` (Port 8005) with Brier calibration tracking.
- Tagged all Futuris forecasting payloads and scenario simulations with `TrustLevel.UNTRUSTED_EXTERNAL`.
- Registered all 7 subsystems (`trading_bot`, `forge`, `nexus`, `sentinel`, `intelx`, `ai_universe`, `friday`) into `EcosystemRegistry`.
- Included IntelX research cancellation in Step 5 of the master emergency halt, preserving partial findings to disk.
- Market research findings are strictly advisory context for risk analysis; automated execution directly based on research is prohibited.
- Confidence-weighted retention decay: high-confidence research (>= 0.90) retained up to 180 days; standard retained 90 days.
- Grounded all factual findings in numerical confidence ratings ($0.0 - 1.0$) and citation counts, separating disputed claims from verified facts.
- Explicitly established Surendra as creator and sole operator in FRIDAY base prompts, removing generic vendor/team references.
