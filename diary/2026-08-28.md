## Key Decisions & Architecture
- Registered all 8 subsystems (`trading_bot`, `forge`, `nexus`, `sentinel`, `intelx`, `futuris`, `ai_universe`, `friday`) into `EcosystemRegistry`.
- Included Futuris forecast subscription cancellation in Step 6 of the master emergency halt cascade.
- Structured daily predictive briefings as dedicated debriefs delivered via voice alongside operational and intelligence briefings.
- Predictions As Decision Inputs: Probabilistic forecasts always inform recommendations without usurping operator or autonomous workflow control.
- Mandatory Confidence Intervals: FRIDAY strictly never returns point predictions without calibrated lower/upper bounds.
- Tagged all Futuris forecasting payloads and scenario simulations with `TrustLevel.UNTRUSTED_EXTERNAL`.
- Confidence-weighted retention decay: high-confidence research (>= 0.90) retained up to 180 days; standard retained 90 days.
- Explicitly established Surendra as creator and sole operator in FRIDAY base prompts, removing generic vendor/team references.
