## Bugs I Found & Fixed
- **Release Panic Substring Collision**: The phrase "release panic" contained "panic", triggering panic activation prematurely.
  - *Fix*: Re-ordered dispatch branches in `TradingBotOperator.execute()` to evaluate `release panic` first.
- **Contested vs Rejected Filter Ambiguity**: The advisory supervisor conflated all rejected advisories with high-confidence contested ones.
  - *Fix*: Separated `monitor_advisories()` (confidence > 0.70) from generic rejected advisory filters.
- **Calendar Briefing Interception**: `MorningBriefingWorkflow` intercepted "Trading morning briefing" queries before skill evaluation.
  - *Fix*: Excluded trading queries in `MorningBriefingWorkflow.can_handle()`.
- **Pytest Class Collection Warnings**: Operator and skill classes starting with `Test...` triggered collection warnings.
  - *Fix*: Added `__test__ = False` to `TestnetAdvisoryOperator` and `TestnetAdvisoryMonitorSkill`.
