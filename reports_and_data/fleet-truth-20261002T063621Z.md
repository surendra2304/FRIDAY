## 4. What this report does not claim

- A `LIVE` verdict means the service answered and labelled its own claim. It does **not** mean the service can perform its task correctly; that needs a functional test against the deployment, which is separate work.
- CI conclusions are read from the local checkout via the version control CLI. A checkout that cannot be queried is recorded as `available: false` rather than assumed green.
- `UNREACHABLE` can mean a cold start on a free tier rather than an outage, so it is separated from `STALE_BUILD` and never merged into it.
